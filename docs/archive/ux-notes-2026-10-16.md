# GreedyGoose: UX notes (2026-10-16)

> **Archived 2026-10-20.** A dated note, kept as written. The product rules live in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md). See [README.md](README.md).

These are improvements we've mapped but not built yet. Each one says what's confusing, why, and how to fix it.
They come from Adrian testing Oulina's account on his iPhone.

## Trade sheet
1. **The price per share sits inside the "You get" amount.**
   - Problem: "$0.00 Cash" sits next to "Price $217.67", which reads as two amounts.
   - Fix: take the price out of the amount block. Put one note line under the cards: "1 NVIDIA = $217.67 · you get at least $X". The amount block then shows only what you get.

## Your credit sheet (Repay | Free up)
2. **"$4.04 of Alphabet backs your credit" next to "Repay $0.89 to get all of it back".**
   - Problem: it reads as if $4.04 is tied to the debt. In fact Aave holds the whole holding, but only about $1 of it is needed for $0.92 owed.
   - Fix:
     - "$4.04 of Alphabet is held for your credit"
     - "You owe $0.92 · repay $0.89 to get all of it back"
3. **The red Watch checkbox in Free up.**
   - Problem: it's big, and it says "keeps my credit in Watch" while the pill above says Safe. It's right (the free-up would drop her into Watch), but it's confusing.
   - Fix: replace it with a small status change next to "You owe after", like "Safe → Watch". Show a warning line only when the result is Watch.
4. **Repay doesn't free a stock.**
   - Problem: users expect a repay to give a stock back.
   - Fix: after a repay, add one line: "You can now get back up to $X of your stocks · Free up" (opens the Free up tab).
5. **Controls that do nothing on small accounts.**
   - Problem: when "Max" is the only pick and it's already selected, or "Cash" is the only choice, a tap changes nothing.
   - Fix: show them as plain text when there's only one option.
6. **The Repay tab scrolls a little**, now that the toggle is there.
   - Fix: trim spacing so the Repay button fits on one screen.

## Repay with stocks
7. **Stocks backing the credit can't repay at the limit.**
   - Problem: Aave checks each step, so the stock can't come out of backing before the repay lands.
   - Fix: repeat take-out → sell → repay a few times in one signature (about 1.3× more each round).
8. **Small free stocks are hidden** (Apple $0.99, NVIDIA $1.00): after the swap they'd bring under the $1 minimum.
   - Fix: lower the minimum, or show the row greyed with the reason. Adrian found the greyed version confusing, so lowering the minimum is the better option.

## Stock sheet
9. **"Free up shares" is buried** under the chart and the stats.
   - Fix: move Sell and Free up right under the holding card. Make the "backs credit" chip tappable so it opens Free up.
10. **Sell is greyed out on a backing stock**, so the user needs two steps.
    - Fix: Sell opens Free up (repay and get back), then the sale. Or, once item 7 is built, "sell and repay" in one step.
11. **"We couldn't load your activity"** shows on the stock sheet. This needs checking on the live site (the history RPC key may be missing).

## Icons and words
12. **Padlock or rings.** The padlock is now used in the Credit sheet's backing rows; the Invest and Home cards still use the rings. Pick one for the whole app.
13. **Never "locked" or "borrow" in the app.**
    - Use: "held for your credit", "backs credit", "free up", "get back".

## JAW
14. **Signing opens a popup on iPhone instead of JAW's iframe**, because greedygoose.vercel.app isn't on JAW's approved list.
    - Fix: ask the JAW team to add the domain. Sign-up stays a popup either way.
